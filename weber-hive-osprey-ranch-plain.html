The Lots That Wait

The short, simple version of the full case file.

What it is

Osprey Ranch is a neighborhood of big lots near Pineview Reservoir in Eden. Phase 1 has 31 lots of 3 to 18 acres. The company selling the lots is Osprey Ranch, LLC.

State business records show who runs that company: Lewis Group Holdings (John Lewis's company) is the manager, and Edenview, Co. (Shane Dunleavy's company) is the member.

What the records show

  • Back taxes: Weber County lists 15 lots as owing $550,079 in property taxes, with interest, as of Oct. 28, 2026. Fourteen belong to Osprey Ranch, LLC. One belongs to Lewis Group Holdings.
  • Since 2023: most of those lots have not had their property taxes paid since 2023.
  • Phase 2: 26 of its 28 lots are still owned by the LLC. Their first tax bills are due Nov. 30, 2026, so none are late yet.
  • Other land: two LLC lots in Legacy Mountain in Huntsville owe $29,157.42 more, unpaid since 2024.
  • Paid at sale: when a lot sells, the title company pays the back taxes at closing. That happened on six lots.
  • Values dropped: in 2026, the lots the developers still held later in the year dropped 13 to 32 percent in value. Lots owned by other buyers stayed the same, except two where homes were built.

Why it matters

Schools, the county, and the fire and water districts count on property taxes each year. When taxes on these lots go unpaid, that money waits until the lots sell. The county does collect it later, with penalties and interest.

Lower values mean lower taxes. The county record does not say why these values dropped.

To be fair: the approvals happened at public meetings with recorded votes, and the developer posted a $3,437,023 guarantee for roads and utilities, per the county staff report.

What we don't know yet

  • Why the 2026 values dropped. It could be an appeal, or how the assessor values unsold lots. Neither is confirmed.
  • When the oldest unpaid taxes reach the four-year point for a tax sale.
  • Why Phase 2 went from 30 lots to 28.

Questions to ask

  • Weber County Assessor: Why did the developers' lots drop in value when other owners' lots did not?
  • Weber County Treasurer: When can the county hold a tax sale on these lots?
  • Weber County Commission: Was the $3,437,023 guarantee posted, and how much has been released?
