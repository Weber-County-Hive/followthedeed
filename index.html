Follow the Deed

Who's actually behind a piece of Utah land, once you trace the LLCs, the registered agents, and the state paperwork all the way down.

Public land → Option agreement → LLC → LLC → Delaware → ?

What this is. Utah's public land — trust land managed for schools, county-owned parcels, state-brokered deals — regularly ends up in the hands of developers who operate through a stack of LLCs, often several layers deep, often routed through Delaware or another state that doesn't require ownership to be disclosed.

Each case file here starts from public records only — the Utah Division of Corporations, state agency board packets, county filings — and follows the chain as far as it can be followed. Where the chain dead-ends, we say so plainly, rather than guessing at who's on the other side.
