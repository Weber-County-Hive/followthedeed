FOLLOW THE DEED · CASE 06 · FD 200.03

The Lots That Wait

At Osprey Ranch in Eden, the developer's unsold lots carry about $550,000 in property taxes listed as unpaid, most of it going back to 2023. When a lot sells, the title company pays the back taxes at closing. And the lots the developers still held in 2026 saw their values drop 13 to 32 percent, while lots owned by other buyers held steady.

Posted Oct. 28, 2026 · Updated Oct. 28, 2026

The short version

  • Osprey Ranch is a large-lot subdivision near Pineview Reservoir in Eden. Its developer, Osprey Ranch, LLC, is managed by John Lewis's company and has Shane Dunleavy's company as its member, per state business records.
  • Weber County lists 15 Phase 1 lots as owing $550,079 in back property taxes with interest, as of Oct. 28, 2026: 14 owned by Osprey Ranch, LLC and one by Lewis Group Holdings. Most have been unpaid since 2023.
  • When a lot sells, the title company pays the back taxes at closing. That happened on six lots, most recently lot 10 on Oct. 5, 2026.
  • Phase 2 (28 lots, on the 2026 tax rolls) has 26 lots still owned by the LLC, valued at $22.5 million together; their first tax bills are due Nov. 30, 2026. Two LLC lots in Legacy Mountain, Huntsville, owe $29,157.42 more, unpaid since 2024.
  • In 2026, lots the developers still held later in the year dropped 13 to 32 percent in value. Lots owned by unrelated buyers did not change, except two with new homes. The county record does not say why.
  • The approvals were made at public meetings with recorded votes, and the developer posted a $3,437,023 improvements guarantee, per the county staff report.

Start here

What is this? Osprey Ranch is a large-lot subdivision on about 560 acres near Pineview Reservoir in Eden. Phase 1 has 31 lots of 3 to 18 acres. The developer is Osprey Ranch, LLC, which state records show is managed by John Lewis's company and has Shane Dunleavy's company as its member.

What did we find? Using only Weber County parcel, tax and plat records, state business filings and public meeting records, we checked every Phase 1 lot. Fourteen lots still owned by Osprey Ranch, LLC and one owned by Lewis Group Holdings are listed as delinquent, most since 2023. The 2026 values on those lots fell sharply. The county record does not say why.

What's the goal? Not to argue against the development. The approvals were made in public, votes are on the record, and the developer posted an improvements guarantee. The question is financial accountability: who pays property taxes on land while it waits to sell, and how values are set on that land.

The short version

$550,079listed as owed on 15 lots, with interest, as of Oct. 28, 2026
2023the first year of unpaid taxes on most of those lots
13–32%drop in 2026 value on lots the developers still held
$0change on lots owned by unrelated buyers (except two that built homes)

What does "delinquent" mean?

Property taxes in Weber County are due Nov. 30. Anything unpaid on Dec. 1 is delinquent and picks up a penalty and interest. The county doesn't lose the money: it stays attached to the land and must be paid before the land can be sold with clear title. Under Utah law, the county can put land up for a tax sale after about four years of unpaid taxes.

What the pattern shows. Every lot that Osprey Ranch, LLC, Lewis Group Holdings or Dunleavy's trust still held later in 2026 dropped 13 to 32 percent. Lots 5 and 26, which the LLC sold in April 2026, kept their values. Lot 30, owned by an out-of-state company, also dropped. Every other lot owned by an unrelated buyer stayed the same, except two where homes were built.
WHAT THE RECORD DOESN'T SAYThe county pages show the new values but not the reason. A value can drop after an owner appeals to the County Board of Equalization, or because of how the assessor values a developer's unsold lots. Neither is confirmed here.

Phase 2 and the LLC's other land

Phase 2 (Liberty side, parcels 22-435-0001 through 22-435-0028). Phase 2 was recorded in 2026 as 28 lots, numbered 32 to 61, plus common areas held by the owners association. These parcels are new on the 2026 tax rolls, so their first tax bills are due Nov. 30, 2026. None are delinquent yet.

  • 26 of the 28 lots are still owned by Osprey Ranch, LLC, with 2026 values from $725,600 to $1,117,800. Together they are valued at $22,499,346.
  • 2 lots are owned by private buyers: one by a deed recorded Feb. 9, 2024, and one by a deed recorded April 24, 2026.
  • The land under Phase 2 came to the LLC by warranty deed recorded May 11, 2021.

Other land owned by Osprey Ranch, LLC. A county owner search for "Osprey Ranch" also lists:

  • Two lots in Legacy Mountain, Huntsville (parcels 20-186-0037 and 20-186-0043), deeded to the LLC by special warranty deed March 8, 2023. Both are listed as delinquent since 2024: $15,835.16 and $13,322.26 with interest, as of Oct. 28, 2026 ($29,157.42 together). Metro National Title paid about $5,400 on each on May 9, 2024. Lot 37's value dropped from $717,311 (2025) to $640,000 (2026); lot 43's did not change.
  • A 15.97-acre parcel (22-040-0044), deeded May 11, 2021. Not delinquent.
  • The 9.21-acre parcel (22-048-0012) whose water is being moved to lot 10. It was first listed as delinquent in 2021; the balance is now paid.
All together: counting the two Legacy Mountain lots, land held by Osprey Ranch, LLC and Lewis Group Holdings is listed as owing $579,236.75 in back property taxes as of Oct. 28, 2026.

Questions to ask, and to whom

  • Ask: Weber County Assessor — Why did the 2026 values on lots still held by Osprey Ranch, LLC, Lewis Group Holdings and the Dunleavy trust drop 13 to 32 percent, while lots owned by unrelated buyers did not change? Was there a Board of Equalization appeal?
  • Ask: Weber County Treasurer — When do the 2023 delinquencies on these lots reach the point where the county can hold a tax sale?
  • Ask: Weber County Commission (public comment, or the Clerk/Auditor) — Was the $3,437,023 Phase 1 improvements guarantee posted, and how much of it has been released?
  • Ask: Weber County Planning — Why did Phase 2 go from 30 lots to 28 between June and October 2025?
  • Ask: Wolf Creek Water and Sewer Improvement District (board meeting public comment) — What land did the district receive on Jan. 26, 2026, and what standby fees apply to Osprey Ranch?
  • Ask: Weber County Treasurer — Will the first-year 2026 taxes on the 26 Phase 2 lots still owned by Osprey Ranch, LLC be paid by Nov. 30, 2026?
